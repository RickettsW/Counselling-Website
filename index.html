About Me

I offer warm, down-to-earth and confidential counselling, where you can talk openly without feeling judged or rushed. I believe that feeling genuinely heard and understood can help people make sense of what they are experiencing and begin to find a way forward.

Alongside my counselling qualifications, I have more than ten years’ experience supporting children, young people and families, including those with ASD and ADHD.

My integrative approach means I draw on a range of therapeutic approaches and tailor our work to what feels right for you. You are the expert in your own life, and I am here to walk alongside you.

How I work

I work in an integrative and person-centred way. Our sessions are shaped around you. I draw on different approaches including person-centred, CBT, narrative, systemic and strengths-based therapy. Together, we will explore what matters to you and find ways to help you move forward at your own pace.

Areas I Can Support With

  • Anxiety, stress and feeling overwhelmed
  • Low mood and depression
  • Self-esteem and confidence issues
  • Bereavement and loss
  • Neurodiversity (ASD & ADHD)
  • Children and young people's emotional wellbeing
